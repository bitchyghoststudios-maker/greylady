KITCHEN

Gas stove, full fridge, cookware and a coffee maker. Wash or load your dishes, and never leave the stove unattended.

BEDROOMS

Five queens

Every bedroom has a queen bed, and the couches take the overflow. Keep to the guest count on your reservation.

BATHROOMS

Three and a half

Old pipes are fussy. Only toilet paper goes in the toilets. Wipes and everything else go in the trash.

HEAT & AIR

Comfortable, not arctic

Reasonable settings keep every floor even. Keep the outside doors closed while it runs.

FIREPLACES

For looks only

They're decorative. No fires, candles or incense anywhere in the house.

ANTIQUES

Easy does it

Don't force old doors, locks or hardware. If something sticks, message us and we'll walk you through it.

LOCKED DOORS

Owner storage

A few doors, including the laundry room and some closets, are locked. Everything you need is open to you.

THE PORCH

Morning coffee or evening drinks. Bring food in when you're done so you don't feed the critters.
